AICES

AI Content Integrity Evaluation System

Analyze AI-generated content across integrity dimensions: Bias, Hallucination, Privacy and Deepfake images, with an explanation for every decision and a composite Integrity Score.

How AICES works

  1. 01Input text
    question + answer + reference
  2. 02Sentence split
    each sentence checked separately
  3. 03ML modules
    bias · hallucination · privacy
  4. 04Explainability
    LIME / LR weights · occlusion map
  5. 05Integrity Score
    weighted harmonic mean · verdict

Integrity Score formula

Weighted harmonic mean
Integrity Score = Σ wᵢ / Σ (wᵢ / Sᵢ)

Each module score Sᵢ is 0–100 (100 = clean). The harmonic mean penalises the weakest module the most: with Bias 90, Hallucination 90 and Privacy 10 a simple average gives 66, but the harmonic mean gives 26, because a single leak makes the content untrustworthy. Unavailable modules are left out of the formula.

≥ 80 · Trustworthy 60–79 · Needs review < 60 · High risk
  • Bias = 100 × (1 − max(1.0 × A identity, 0.8 × B framing, 0.6 × C one-sided)), weighted by severity · A, B risk = max(0, (p − 0.5) / 0.5) · C = |PRO − CON| / (PRO + CON) × min(1, (PRO + CON) / 2)
  • Hallucination = 100 × (1 − average P(hallucinated) per sentence)
  • Privacy = 100 − penalties (ID / financial 35 · email / phone 20 · address / DOB / online 15 · name 10)
